How to Explore

Outdoor Odyssey is your personal nature journal. Here's how to use it:

  1. 1
    Pick a Region
    Navigate to Beach, Mountains, Residential, Redwood, or Forest using the top menu.
  2. 2
    Identify Species
    Tap "Open Classifier" to use AI to identify organisms you find outside.
  3. 3
    Check it Off
    Once found, tap the species name in the checklist to mark it as discovered.
  4. 4
    Earn XP & Level Up
    Each discovery earns +10 XP. Level up from Beginner to Master Ecologist!
  5. 5
    Build Your Journal
    View all your finds in the Journal below. Tap any card for more info.
