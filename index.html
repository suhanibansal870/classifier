How to Use Outdoor Odyssey

  1. Select your designated region from the navigation bar or menu
  2. Use “Open Classifier” to identify organisms listed in the checklist
  3. Use the checklist to check off the species you discovered
  4. Earn XP as you explore
  5. View your findings in the Journal section and learn more about them
