Identification Adventure!

Would you like to enhance your experience in nature? If you do, this is the right place for you! This website links image classifiers for beaches, mountains, residential areas, and forests! Click on the buttons below to access it, depending on where you are. Once you do, you will be able to take pictures of natural habitats for the species listed in the bottom panel, to find some of the most common ones in your designated area!


Beach Species Identification

Species to identify: barnacles, flowers, grass, kelp, mussels, primroses, sage bushes, seagulls, and seashells!


Mountain Species Identification

Species to identify: bird, lizard, pine, squirrel, wildflower



Residential Species Identification

Species to identify: bird, squirrel, bee, daisy, ivy, ladybug, rose, spider


Redwood Species Identification

Species to identify: banana slug, bird, butterfly, fern, moss

Forest Species Identification

Species to identify: ant, bird, fern, moss, poison oak, snail, squirrel, tree, wildflower
